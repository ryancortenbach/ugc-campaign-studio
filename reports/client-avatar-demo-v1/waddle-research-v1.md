Hypothesis: adult dating couples who want a lightweight daily connection ritual, especially geographically separated college or early-career couples. Buyer: founder-led consumer-app team testing creative.

- Evidence: Official site supports couples together or at a distance.

## Proposed original storyboard

![Four-shot concept](/client-demo-assets/waddle-storyboard-v1.png)

15 seconds is a target. Speech timing and motion have not been verified. Actual product UI must be composited from permitted genuine footage.

- **0-3 sec**: Still asking, "How was your day?" Camera: Vertical close selfie at cafe table. Action: Raised eyebrow, natural candid delivery; no creator identity or voice reuse.
- **3-7 sec**: Waddle gives you better questions to ask together. Camera: Same scene, slightly wider medium. Action: Small open-hand gesture; genuine approved question-card footage goes in edit.
- **7-12 sec**: Share a photo, raise a duck, make time for each other. Camera: Same camera axis and charcoal hoodie. Action: Gesture toward separately composited genuine duck/photo recording. No generated app screen.
- **12-15 sec**: Try a daily check-in with Waddle. Camera: Return to close selfie. Action: Friendly small smile; simple brand/URL end card added in edit.
