## Proposed original storyboard

![Four-shot concept](/client-demo-assets/awardscholar-storyboard-v1.png)

15 seconds is a target. Speech timing and motion have not been verified. Actual product UI must be composited from permitted genuine footage.

- **0-3 sec**: Scholarship search taking over your weekend? Camera: Vertical chest-up, same desk and camera axis. Action: Eyebrow lift and direct eye contact; avoid a fabricated personal success story.
- **3-7 sec**: Award Scholar matches opportunities to your profile Camera: Same scene, medium framing. Action: Small open-hand gesture. Later composite a genuine approved matching-screen capture.
- **7-11 sec**: and helps draft applications you can review. Camera: Same camera and wardrobe. Action: Indicate space beside presenter for genuine editable-draft footage; no generated UI.
- **11-15 sec**: Check your matches, read each provider's rules, and start there. Camera: Return to chest-up. Action: Small smile and plain CTA; app name and URL composited later.
