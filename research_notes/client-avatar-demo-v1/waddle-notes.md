## Original creative proposal

Persona: Jamie, a fictional adult around 24, with wavy dark hair, small round glasses, a sage sweatshirt and charcoal relaxed trousers. Warm and lightly playful, distinct from Donald and Xander. Use an original voice, not a founder clone. Lock the same face, glasses, hair, clothes, room and dusk lighting across frames.

Environment: cozy apartment couch, cream wall, small amber lamp, one houseplant, neutral smartphone prop. Composite actual permitted Waddle screen recordings later. Do not generate fake app UI or logos in the image model.

Proposed 15-second script, 33 words:

| Time | Picture | Dialogue |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Jamie chest-up, speaking to lens | Still asking, "How was your day?" |
| 3-7s | Same character leans in; verified app insert | Waddle gives you better questions to ask together. |
| 7-12s | Same character gestures toward shared-duck app insert | Share a photo, raise a duck, make a little time for each other. |
| 12-15s | Warm smile; name and website composited later | Try a daily check-in with Waddle. |

This is an original proposal based on product features, not a reconstruction of Xander's media. Label the sample as an illustrative AI character. It does not claim Jamie is a real user or that Waddle cures relationship problems. Human approval remains required for character, frames, claims, assets and eventual production/send.
