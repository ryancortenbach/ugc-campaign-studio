# Content to commerce sales playbook

Research checked October 24, 2026. Strategy and launch offers below are proposals. No private sales data was obtained.

## What is publicly observable

Children of Khan connects narrative and meme collections to an apparel catalog. Its homepage currently lists many tees around $60, alongside higher-priced layers, a shipping incentive above $90, a buy-three-get-one promotion, and a newsletter. These observations describe the visible offer, not its sales volume or profitability. Source: [Children of Khan](https://www.childrenofkhan.com/).

## Our customer path

An original recurring character makes a recognizable observation about gaming, online life, clothes, or relationships. The product belongs naturally in that character's scene. A single call to action sends the viewer to the relevant offer, rather than asking every viewer to buy all three products.

1. Viewers who want the clothes land on the exact collection or product shown in the clip.
2. Creators who ask how the video was made land on the $97 course demonstration and curriculum.
3. Brand operators who want content for their business land on the avatar service, inspect a sample, and request a scoped pilot.

Keep separate landing links and campaign tags for each route. Attribute sales using checkout events and order records. Public view counts cannot replace this measurement.

## Apparel launch

Start with three original print directions and one recurring character world. Test $59.99 tees, the proposed four-for-three bundle, and free shipping above $90. The preview cart applies the cheapest free item for each four eligible tees and evaluates the threshold after discounts. Those are our proposed implementation rules, not a claim about Khan's checkout internals.

At the preview's equal price, four tees total $179.97 before taxes. A full bundle discounts the four-unit sticker price by 25 percent. Fulfillment still pays for all four garments. The existing economics panel can represent this by setting units per order to 4 and average discount to 25 percent. For mixed baskets, use measured average units and effective discount. Do not apply 25 percent to every order without evidence.

Before accepting real orders, select blanks, print method, landed costs, size chart, returns policy, inventory or made-to-order delivery times, and the actual checkout. No artificial countdowns, fabricated scarcity, invented reviews, or borrowed customer counts.

## Course sales page

Show a real example and explain the production steps: character definition, reference binding, scene planning, voice, generation, continuity review, editing, and publishing preparation. Offer templates, a worked project, and the prompt-review checklist for $97. Publish accurate module contents and access terms before charging. A software mock alone is not a completed course.

Use the original character's production breakdown as the demonstration. Show actual costs and mistakes once measured. Promise the learning materials and workflow delivered, with no income or audience guarantee. The Whop checkout remains pending the real product link.

## For Brands sales motion

Research a relevant product and its actual customer. Prepare an unsent, specific message and a five-second sample concept in the prospect's visual world. Ask for a small pilot with agreed deliverables, review rounds, usage rights, and a spending cap. Use the accepted pilot to scope a recurring package.

Ten-plus daily posts is a service target, contingent on production capacity, approved assets, platform access, and a real generation budget. Define length, channels, approval turnaround, reuse, and revisions. Guarantee only controllable deliverables supported by the contract and working system. Never guarantee views, leads, revenue, or return on ad spend.

## Experiments and measurement

Compare one variable at a time: character hook, first scene, product integration, call to action, or landing offer. Keep the source clip, prompt version, review verdict, generation spend, and destination tag together.

Track publication count, retention, profile visits, link clicks, product-page visits, add-to-cart rate, checkout completion, units per order, discount, refunds, fulfillment cost, and contribution margin. For services, also track replies, qualified calls, pilots, closes, review time, generation acceptance, and client retention. Required sample sizes depend on traffic and uncertainty. Do not declare a winner from a single viral video.

## Financial calibration

The current calculator is a sensitivity model, not Khan's reported results. Replace placeholder conversion rates, average views, landed costs, generation yield, and labor with observed data. Keep apparel and course acquisition costs shared only when the same content actually serves both, while charging each brand client for their separate production workload.

The next calibration milestone is a measured, attributed pilot: approved outputs, real generation spend, production time, clicks, orders or qualified inquiries, refunds, and actual net payout. Until those exist, the honest output is a range of scenarios, not a sales forecast.
