Generation and review rules

Genjutsu swaps clothes and preserves source audio. Brand names and changed joke lines need a separate approved voice and lip-sync stage. Use six short source clips, each below the current 15-second API limit.

AI reviews the stills, then Ryan or a named creative reviewer approves the concept. A current quote and budget approval come before paid production. After generation, AI inspects the frames and joins, and a human watches the complete video with sound.

Terac can collect human feedback using the saved review questions. A Terac study is not launched here. Store respondent identity, scores and specific corrections; final creative acceptance belongs to the authorized reviewer.

Any script, garment reference or output change invalidates the corresponding approval. Fix failures and return to the affected review stage.

Full state machine and approval contract
Reply handling and CRM

After an authorized send, save the actual provider message and thread IDs. When reply monitoring is enabled, dedupe incoming messages and log the original reply, intent, stage, owner and next action in the CRM.

Interest becomes a discovery reply or call invitation using actual availability. Price and scope questions become a deal draft within approved terms. A booked call is distinct from an accepted offer or confirmed payment.

Out-of-office replies do not count as interest. Negative replies and opt-outs stop the follow-up sequence. This package does not activate sending or reply monitoring.

AI self-review and remaining gaps

Character: Recognizable face, blond braids, skin tone and PS2 style carried into both generated boards. Fine facial geometry is not pixel-identical.

Shot coverage: Six readable key poses: greeting, rise setup, standing reveal, garment view, explanation gesture and downward CTA. These are stills, so movement and temporal continuity remain untested.

Apparel visibility: Full-body panels show top and pants. Gestures in the medium panels keep most chest details exposed.

Same garment within a column: Black tee/charcoal pants and cream crewneck/light pants appear consistent across the two views in each brand concept.

Logo and product accuracy: BLOCK for production. Gymshark mark is a generated approximation rather than the requested wordmark. Alo mark is also generated. Neither is bound to exact product references.

Room continuity: Room palette, chair and skyline carry through. Window geometry and furniture perspective need checks at actual clip joins; no exact scene preservation claimed.

Hands: No obvious gross hand defect at contact-sheet scale. Full-resolution individual frames and moving occlusions still require production review.

Pose continuity: Rise is split across shots 02 and 03. Need matched final/first frames and foot contact when animating.

Dialogue: Brand greetings and two joke lines differ; shared copy does not assume product performance or that current Meta ads were observed.

Audio and timing: Not generated or heard. Timing, pronunciation, voice identity, lip-sync and music require full human playback approval.
