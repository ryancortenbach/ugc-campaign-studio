FORMULA FLOW
Own-channel monthly posts = days * posts per day.
Views = posts * average views per post. This is a scenario, not a prediction.
Visits = views * view-to-site rate. Tee/course traffic allocation shares are non-overlapping and must total no more than 100%.
Orders = allocated visits * stream-specific conversion.
Tee gross = orders * (unit selling price * units/order * (1-discount) + shipping charged).
Tee net = gross less expected refunds. COGS and shipping apply to all fulfilled orders, a conservative assumption without recovery value for returned goods.
Payment fees apply to gross payments, with fixed fee per order. Affiliate commissions apply to net retained revenue for the affiliate share of sales.
Course contribution = retained course revenue less payment fees, affiliate fees, and per-order support.
Expected paid generation per approved post = seconds * cost/second * (1-reuse fraction) / first-pass acceptance yield. Independent retries/geometric success is an assumption, not a guarantee. Reuse can reduce generation needs but does not eliminate review, voice, edit, or LLM costs.
Shared own-brand content cost is deducted once across the tee and course channels. This avoids charging the same marketing content twice.
Each brand client incurs its own daily post production and support costs. Client revenue is reduced for refunds, processing and expected churn-replacement CAC.
Operating profit before tax = all stream contributions - shared content cost - shared overhead, including imputed labor, course setup amortization, prospect research and sample production.
First-month cash proxy deducts the upfront course setup instead of amortization. It is not a tax or complete accounting cashflow statement. Working-capital proxy = cash operating costs/day * payout delay days.
Break-even outputs report units or clients at positive contribution margins. Nonpositive unit margin has no finite break-even.

REQUIRED SENSITIVITIES
Views and click-through can fail independently. Test low audience reach, poor conversion, 50% generation yield, doubled generation price, higher refunds, higher shipping, affiliate acquisition, client churn, and 10 vs 15 posts/day. Count 5-second samples as an acquisition cost even when no prospect buys.

WHAT CAN BE PROMISED
A defined production cadence, scope, review process, retry budget, reporting, and support response time can become service commitments after demonstrated capacity and platform access. Rejected and failed generations are not delivered posts. No guaranteed views, virality, follower count, conversion, sales, revenue, or profit follows from another creator's performance. A content-volume SLA needs a quality definition, exclusions, account access, and remedy for missed delivery.
